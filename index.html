// fragment II — alternate world protocol
MAYBE?
nine fragments. one question. no answer.
I
Maybe he makes everything work
in an alternate reality.
II
Maybe he didn't wish to end it all.
III
Maybe his voice doesn't falter, his hands don't shake, his mind doesn't loop back to all the ways he failed.
IV
his hands weren't made for trembling, weren't made for breaking things he never learned to hold.
V
Maybe he hated himself a little less.
VI
Maybe he walks through doors without hesitating, without feeling like an apology wrapped in skin.
VII
Maybe the world is gentle there.
Maybe loss is not a lesson but a myth, and love is not something time can steal.
VIII
Maybe what he loves stays.
Maybe he stays, too.
∞
Maybe?
// fragment III — what was done to the bloom
you broke me
like a bloom
you feared to water.
held
petals
like
proof
you broke me like a bloom you feared to water,   held petals in your palm like proof instead of love.
now you gather what you shattered, hoping wilt can turn to spring   but every piece remembers the tearing.
hold me and bleed. hold me, and regret.   for flowers do not rise for hands that pulled them apart.
you broke me like a bloom you never meant to water.   you kept the petals as proof, not love.   now you want me living again   but flowers don't rise for hands that pulled them apart.
it came to me   i held it like something that could stay   when it went still i blamed my hands
// fragment V — the boy, the house, the measurements
i wish you were gentle
to the boy i was
before
the loops
before
the words
he slept with his dreams folded beneath his ribs, hopes pressed flat so the world wouldn't hear them crack. i wish you saw them. i wish you saw the man i became.
you talk about my failures like a morning prayer mouth full of my name, hands clean of blame, as if shame were a ritual that could rinse me into worth. a dead son is not better than a failed one. sometimes i wish i were, because i am tired of surviving
this house knows my measurements love stops just short of me, and i am always one breath from enough.
